Background

About Me

I’m an electrical engineer focused on RF and microwave systems. I graduated from The University of Alabama in May 2026 with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering, and I now work as a graduate RF engineering intern at QuadSAT in Odense, Denmark.

Most of my work is radar hardware for remote sensing: taking an RF system from architecture and component selection through PCB fabrication, integration and field deployment on UAS platforms, across the UHF, Ku and Ka bands. I also work on the embedded control and power electronics that turn an RF chain into a complete payload.

My advisors are Dr. Prasad Gogineni and Dr. Todd Freeborn.
